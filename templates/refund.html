Thank you for choosing TRAGENE Journal ("Company", "we", "our", or "us").

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which refunds may or may not be granted for purchases made through the TRAGENE Journal website, dashboard, applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. General Policy

TRAGENE Journal offers digital subscription-based software services.

Due to the nature of digital products and the immediate availability of premium features after purchase, all purchases are generally final and non-refundable unless otherwise stated in this Refund Policy.

2. Subscription Plans

We offer subscription plans that may include:

  • Monthly Plans
  • Yearly Plans
  • Promotional Plans
  • Future Subscription Plans introduced by TRAGENE Journal

Subscription prices, features, and availability are displayed on the official pricing pages, homepage, and user dashboard.

3. No Automatic Renewal

TRAGENE Journal does not automatically renew subscriptions.

When your subscription expires:

  • Your payment method will not be charged automatically.
  • Your subscription will expire at the end of its billing period.
  • If you wish to continue using premium features, you must manually purchase a new subscription.

Because there is no automatic renewal, refunds are not provided for unintended renewals.

4. Cancellation Policy

Once a subscription has been successfully purchased and activated:

  • It cannot be cancelled before its expiration date.
  • Access to premium features will remain available until the subscription period ends.
  • Stopping use of the Service does not cancel the subscription or create eligibility for a refund.

5. Switching Subscription Plans

Users may choose to purchase a different subscription plan at any time.

Purchasing a different plan:

  • Does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.
  • Does not create eligibility for a refund on any previous purchase.
  • Does not entitle the user to partial refunds or unused subscription credits unless expressly approved by TRAGENE Journal.

6. When Refunds Are Not Available

Refunds will generally not be provided for reasons including, but not limited to:

  • Change of mind
  • Accidental purchase
  • Failure to use the Service
  • Lack of understanding of platform features
  • Dissatisfaction with personal trading performance
  • Trading losses
  • User configuration issues
  • Incompatible devices not listed as supported
  • Failure to read product descriptions
  • Subscription expiration
  • Failure to cancel before expiration (where applicable)
  • User preference changes
  • Internet connectivity issues on the user's side
  • Broker-related issues
  • Third-party trading platform issues
  • Third-party AI service interruptions beyond our reasonable control

7. Refund Eligibility

A refund may be considered only if TRAGENE Journal determines that the Service could not be provided due to a verified issue directly caused by our systems.

Examples may include:

  • Prolonged server outage preventing normal use of the Service
  • Verified payment processing errors resulting in duplicate charges
  • Technical issues caused solely by our platform that permanently prevent access to purchased features
  • Billing errors made by TRAGENE Journal
  • Other exceptional circumstances determined solely by TRAGENE Journal

Each request is individually reviewed.

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval.

8. Technical Issue Investigation

Before approving any refund request, we may investigate the reported issue.

This investigation may include reviewing:

  • Server logs
  • Authentication records
  • Payment records
  • Error reports
  • System activity
  • Subscription status
  • User account activity
  • Technical diagnostics

You may be asked to provide additional information to assist in the investigation.

9. Duplicate Payments

If you are charged more than once for the same subscription due to a verified billing or payment processing error, we may, after verification:

  • Refund the duplicate payment, or
  • Credit the duplicate amount toward a future subscription, at our discretion.

10. Fraud Prevention

To protect our users and the integrity of the Service, refund requests may be declined if we reasonably believe that:

  • False information has been provided
  • Payment fraud has occurred
  • Unauthorized activity has taken place
  • The Service has been intentionally misused
  • Refund requests are abusive or repetitive

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts involved in fraudulent refund activity.

11. Payment Provider Processing

Approved refunds, when applicable, will generally be processed through the original payment method used for the purchase.

Actual processing times may vary depending on the payment provider, banking institution, or payment network.

TRAGENE Journal is not responsible for delays caused by third-party payment providers after a refund has been initiated.

12. Taxes and Fees

Any taxes, duties, or applicable governmental charges associated with your purchase may be handled according to applicable laws and payment provider policies.

Where permitted by law, certain taxes or transaction fees may not be refundable.

13. Modifications to This Policy

TRAGENE Journal reserves the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time.

Any updates will become effective upon publication on the official website unless otherwise stated.

14. Contact Us

If you believe you are eligible for a refund under this Refund Policy, please contact us through the official support channels listed on our website.

When submitting a request, please include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Transaction ID (if available)
  • Date of purchase
  • Description of the issue
  • Screenshots or supporting information, if applicable

Providing complete information helps us review your request more efficiently.

15. Acceptance of This Policy

By purchasing any subscription, paid feature, or digital service offered by TRAGENE Journal, you acknowledge that:

  • You have read and understood this Refund Policy.
  • You understand that subscriptions are generally non-refundable.
  • You understand that subscriptions are not automatically renewed.
  • You understand that you must manually purchase a new subscription after expiration if you wish to continue using premium features.
  • You understand that refunds are only considered in limited circumstances involving verified issues caused directly by TRAGENE Journal.

Your continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of this Refund Policy.

By purchasing any subscription or paid feature, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund Policy.
