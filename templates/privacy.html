1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information generated while using the Service, and certain information collected automatically.

1.1 Account Information

When creating an account, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Username
  • Email Address
  • Password (stored securely in hashed form)
  • Profile Photo (if uploaded)
  • Country
  • Time Zone
  • Preferred Language
  • Account Preferences

1.2 Trading Information

If you connect a supported trading platform or upload trading information, we may collect:

  • Trading Account Number
  • Broker Name
  • Trading Platform Information
  • Account Type
  • Account Currency
  • Trade History
  • Orders
  • Open Positions
  • Closed Positions
  • Trade Entry Price
  • Trade Exit Price
  • Stop Loss
  • Take Profit
  • Trade Volume
  • Profit and Loss
  • Commission
  • Swap
  • Balance History
  • Equity History
  • Margin Information
  • Drawdown Statistics
  • Win Rate
  • Risk Metrics
  • Performance Statistics
  • Tragene Journal Notes
  • Uploaded Trade Screenshots
  • Tags and Categories
  • Custom Notes

We only collect this information when you voluntarily connect your trading account or upload data.

1.3 AI Feature Data

If you use AI-powered features, we may process information including:

  • Trading history
  • Trading statistics
  • Journal entries
  • Notes
  • Performance metrics
  • Uploaded screenshots
  • Questions submitted to AI
  • AI-generated reports and summaries

This processing enables features such as:

  • Trade analysis
  • Performance insights
  • Pattern recognition
  • Risk evaluation
  • Trading psychology summaries
  • Personalized analytics
  • Journal assistance

AI-generated responses are informational only and should not be considered financial or investment advice.

1.4 Payment Information

When purchasing a subscription, payments are processed by our payment provider.

We do not store your complete payment card details.

Depending on the payment provider, we may receive:

  • Purchase Amount
  • Currency
  • Payment Date

When using the Service, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Type

Your trading data is processed solely to provide the functionality of TRAGENE Journal.

  • Trade Journaling
  • AI Analysis
  • Risk Analysis
  • Performance Reports
  • Charts
  • Statistics
  • Trading Insights

  • Dashboard Metrics

Some features rely on Artificial Intelligence.

  • Analytics providers
  • AI service providers

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights, users, or the security of the Service.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including:

  • HTTPS encryption
  • Password hashing
  • Secure authentication
  • Access controls
  • Server security
  • Database protection
  • Security monitoring
  • Regular backups
  • Protection against unauthorized access

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as reasonably necessary to:

If you delete your account, your personal information and trading data will be removed from our active systems within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or where limited backup copies remain temporarily for disaster recovery purposes.
