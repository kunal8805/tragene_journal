What Is a Trading Journal?

A trading journal is more than a logbook. It's your personal performance database — recording every entry, exit, profit, loss, and the reasoning behind each trade. Professional traders don't guess what's working. They know, because their journal tells them.

Without a journal, you're trading on memory. And memory is unreliable. You might remember the big win on gold last week, but forget the three small losses on EURUSD that erased half of it. A proper trading journal shows you the complete picture.

📊 Did You Know?

Studies show that traders who maintain a detailed journal improve their win rate by 15-25% within 3 months. The act of recording forces you to justify every trade — and that alone filters out bad setups.

But a spreadsheet isn't enough anymore. Modern trading journals like Tragene Journal (available at TrageneJournal.com) use AI to analyze your patterns, auto-sync trades from your broker, and give you specific, actionable feedback — not just numbers in a grid.
