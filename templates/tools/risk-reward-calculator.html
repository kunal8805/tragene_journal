📖 How Risk Reward Works

The risk:reward ratio tells you how much you stand to gain vs how much you could lose.

Formula:

Risk = |Entry - Stop Loss|

Reward = |Take Profit - Entry|

R:R Ratio = Reward / Risk

A ratio of 1:2 or higher is generally considered good (you stand to gain twice what you risk).
