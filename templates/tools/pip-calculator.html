📖 How Pips Work

A pip is the smallest price movement in forex trading.

  • Standard pairs (EURUSD): 0.0001 = 1 pip
  • JPY pairs (USDJPY): 0.01 = 1 pip
  • Gold (XAUUSD): 0.01 = 1 pip
  • Indices (US30): 1.00 = 1 pip
