Why Traders Switch

Built the way serious traders actually work

Two things separate a real Tragene Journal from a glorified spreadsheet: an AI that's actually usable without paying first, and sync that doesn't force you to pick a side between MT4 and MT5.

AI Coaching, Even On The Free Plan

Most Tragene Journals lock every AI feature behind a paywall, so you never actually find out if the analysis is any good before you pay for it. Tragene Journal takes a different approach: every Free account gets a real weekly AI token allowance, enough to run an AI diary entry or a quick analysis pass without touching a card.

That makes it one of the few AI-powered journals where the AI isn't a locked demo — it's a genuine part of the free product, upgraded to an advanced model and much higher limits once you move to Pro or Elite.

MT4 And MT5 Sync, Not Just One

A lot of traders run more than one terminal — an MT4 account with an old broker, an MT5 account for everything else. Tragene Journal's auto sync is fully compatible with both MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5, so switching platforms never means switching journals.

Connect an account once and every closed trade lands in your journal automatically, with entries, exits, size, and P&L already filled in — nothing to retype from a broker statement.

One Journal, Every Session

Whether you trade gold during the London open, indices through New York, or crypto around the clock, Tragene Journal is built to sit underneath all of it. Manual logging stays instant for traders who like typing their own notes, while MT4/MT5 auto sync handles the accounts you'd rather not babysit.

The result is a single, unified history — not four spreadsheets and a broker export you forgot to update.

The AI layer is designed to be genuinely useful rather than decorative: it reads your actual entries and notes, not just your P&L, and calls out things like oversized risk after a loss or a string of trades taken outside your usual session.

Free traders get a real taste of that with a weekly AI allowance. Pro and Elite traders get the advanced models, higher limits, and the full AI coach and goals planner on top.
