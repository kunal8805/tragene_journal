📖 How Position Sizing Works

Position sizing determines how many lots you should trade based on your risk tolerance.

Formula:

Risk Amount = Account Balance × Risk %

Position Size (lots) = Risk Amount / (Stop Loss × Pip Value)

Where pip value depends on the currency pair type.
